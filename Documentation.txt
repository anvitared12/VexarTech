Other than the two dashboards we can find out about these factors:

From Odometer value:
Fuel Efficiency of Vehicle : Tracking distance against fuel fill-ups lets you calculate true miles-per-gallon (MPG) to
spot engine performance drops.

From Manufacture Year:
Actual Age vs. Paper Age: A car built in November might be registered in January of the next year; the paperwork says
it is newer, but the rubber, battery, and fluid components have been aging on a lot

From Make and Model:
From this we can collect data from internet on difference between build of vehicles which could give us information
drawbacks of particular vehicle.

From Latitude and Longitude:
Geographic Mapping (Latitude, Longitude): Traces the exact physical route on a map.

Taking Registered date and accordingly keeping records for vehicles pollution test and servicing will help in taking care
of vehicles in long run.

By using Gyrometer and Accelerometer we can predict weather the driver has been in accident.
